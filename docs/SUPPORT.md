# Droxion Fit support

Droxion Fit supports manual meal logging, grocery lists with entered prices, and beginner home workouts. Photo and barcode scanning are not included in this release.

## Log a meal

Complete onboarding, choose Add meal on Today, enter the meal name and nutrition from your food label, and save. Tap a saved meal to edit it.

## Droxion Plus

Plus includes meal history, seven-day nutrition progress and home workouts. The monthly subscription price and billing terms appear in the Apple or Google purchase sheet before you authorize payment.

If the price cannot load, check your internet connection and tap Try loading price again. Loading a price does not start a purchase. If you already subscribed, use Restore purchases with the same store account. Manage or cancel through your Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions settings. Do not buy again just because access has not refreshed.

## Saved data

Meal diary, grocery items and workout history are stored on your device. Resetting local data removes those records; it does not cancel a store subscription.

## Contact

Email support@droxion.com with your device type, app version and a short description. Do not include passwords, payment information or sensitive health data. You can also report a technical issue using this repository's Issues tab without posting personal information.
