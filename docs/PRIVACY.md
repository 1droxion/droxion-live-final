# Droxion Fit privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026.

## Scope

This release supports manual meal logging, grocery lists and home workouts. It does not upload meal photos for AI analysis or provide photo/barcode scanning. It does not require contacts, precise location, microphone access or a social account.

## Local records

Your profile, meal diary, entered nutrition values, groceries, entered prices, budget and workout completions are stored locally on your device. Any meal thumbnails retained from older releases stay local until deleted or local data is reset. Sharing a grocery list sends its text through the sharing app you choose.

## Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. Droxion Fit receives product and verified purchase/entitlement information needed to unlock Plus; it does not receive your full payment card details. iOS subscription access uses native StoreKit verification. A random installation identifier may be passed to the store to associate a transaction with the app installation.

## Analytics

The app may send limited product events, such as onboarding completion, paywall views and subscription events, to improve the product. These events are not used for medical diagnosis. Legacy scanning events may exist from older releases; this release does not provide scanning.

## Control of your data

Use the app's local reset control to remove your profile and activity records from that device. This does not cancel subscriptions or delete store transaction records held by Apple or Google. Store billing and cancellation are managed through your store account.

## Nutrition estimates

Targets and entered or database nutrition values may be inaccurate. Droxion Fit provides general wellness tracking, not medical advice.

## Contact

Privacy questions: support@droxion.com. Do not post sensitive health information in public repository issues.
